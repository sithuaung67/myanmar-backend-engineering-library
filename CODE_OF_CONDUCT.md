
# 📜 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone.

Regardless of experience level, gender, age, nationality, religion, disability, or background, every contributor deserves respect.

Our goal is to build knowledge together.

---

# 🤝 Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful
- Being patient
- Being constructive
- Helping newcomers
- Accepting feedback professionally
- Sharing knowledge openly
- Respecting different opinions

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment
- Hate speech
- Personal attacks
- Discrimination
- Trolling
- Spamming
- Sharing private information
- Abusive language

---

# 💬 Communication

When giving feedback:

✔ Explain why.

✔ Suggest improvements.

✔ Be kind.

Remember:

Criticize ideas, not people.

---

# 🌱 Community Values

We believe:

Knowledge grows when it is shared.

Everyone starts as a beginner.

Learning never stops.

Quality matters.

Community comes first.

---

# 📬 Reporting

If you experience unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer.

Every report will be reviewed fairly and respectfully.

---

Thank you for helping build a healthy Myanmar developer community.
