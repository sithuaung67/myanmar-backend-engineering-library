# Project Principles

## 1. Quality Over Quantity

We prioritize accuracy over speed.

---

## 2. Official Documentation First

Every technical topic should be reviewed against official documentation.

---

## 3. Explain Why Before How

We teach reasoning before implementation.

---

## 4. Production Before Tutorial

Examples should reflect real-world engineering practices.

---

## 5. Community Before Ego

Every contribution is welcome.
Ideas are reviewed based on technical merit.

---

## 6. Keep Learning

Technology changes.

Engineering principles remain.
